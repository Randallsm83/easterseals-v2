# Behavioral Research

Researchers study participant responses under configured reward conditions.

## Language

**Session**:
A separately recorded period in which one participant responds under one configuration. Sessions remain individually analyzable when included in a session sequence.

**Session sequence**:
A named, reusable ordered list of saved configurations, with repeats allowed. A researcher selects a participant when launching it.
_Avoid_: A single session with phases

**Sequence run**:
One participant’s execution of a session sequence using the conditions fixed at launch. Its sessions remain separately recorded and advance immediately without participant-facing transition screens.

**Sequence balance**:
The first session’s starting balance plus rewards earned across a sequence run. Later sessions’ starting balances are local to those sessions and do not increase the sequence balance.

**Interrupted sequence run**:
A sequence run stopped before all its sessions finish. Saved results remain available; further participation requires a deliberately started new run rather than automatic replay, skipping, or resumption.
